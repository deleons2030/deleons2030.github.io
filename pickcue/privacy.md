# PickCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

PickCue is designed as a local-only decision helper.

## Data Collection

PickCue does not collect personal data.

The choices you enter and the pick history you save are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to PickCue, a server, or any third party.

## Tracking

PickCue does not track you across apps or websites.

## Accounts

PickCue does not require an account or sign-in.

## Network Access

PickCue does not use network access for its core features.

## Third Parties

PickCue does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, or third-party login SDKs.

## Permissions

PickCue does not request access to camera, photos, location, contacts, microphone, notifications, Bluetooth, or local network.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the support channel listed on the App Store product page or visit https://deleons2030.github.io/.
